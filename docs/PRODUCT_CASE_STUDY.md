# Accountable matter review

## Product question

Can a team reduce the time spent reconstructing why a draft says something while catching material omissions before handoff?

The revised Law Suite centers on an issue, its evidence, uncertainty, owner, and recorded decision. Firm Operations remains an explicitly illustrative supporting surface.

## Review logic

Each issue has category, severity, owner, source/version, excerpt, evidence state, next step, review status, and reviewer note. Source states distinguish available, absent, conflicting, and outdated material. Availability permits a user-recorded review; it does not prove the finding or authenticate the reviewer.

The second demo iteration separates evidence state, assigned work, and internal approval. Handoff requires current reviews, supported evidence for included propositions, resolved assignments, recorded prerequisites, and a valid internal target. Unsupported propositions can be explicitly excluded with a reason and reviewed as limitations; their evidence remains unverified. Source revisions and draft edits reopen dependent reviews and revoke approval. Browser-local progress survives refresh. HTML memorandum and JSON exports retain open findings, sources, qualifications, and simulated participant history. These are editable demo records, not immutable audits or filing authorization.

## Product decisions

- Show the source alongside the action, without an invented confidence score.
- Keep missing evidence and contradictions visible and blocking.
- Require a named reviewer and substantive note, while disclosing that identity and quality are not verified.
- Treat the next stage as supervising-lawyer review, not client delivery.
- Show value assumptions including human review effort and negative results.
- Disclose the synthetic boundary and disconnected services in the interface.

## Discovery to conduct

Observe appropriately authorized partner/associate review sessions. Interview supervising partners, knowledge lawyers, risk teams, pricing leaders, and IT owners. Validate which issues are hardest to trace, when source changes invalidate review, how client restrictions create stops, what belongs in a Word/DMS handoff, and how much drafting time is consumed by verification.

No customer interviews were performed in this update. These are hypotheses informed by public sources in [the assessment](LAW_SUITE_REVIEW.md).

## Evaluation

Use matched permissioned tasks, blinded lawyer grading, equivalent source access, and held-out matters. Measure material errors/omissions, citation support, adverse-authority recall, total review/rework time, and cost per accepted deliverable. Report failures and uncertainty. Adoption metrics do not establish legal quality.
